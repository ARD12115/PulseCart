## Brand & Style

Architectural Minimalism for e-commerce: clinical, high-speed, structurally disciplined. Hairline boundaries, tabular truth, restrained functional chroma.

## Layout

- Desktop (1280px+): 12-column grid, gutter-lg (2rem), margin-lg (3rem), max container 1440px
- Tablet (768–1279px): 8-column grid, gutter (1.5rem), margin (2rem)
- Mobile (<768px): 4-column grid, gutter-sm (1rem), margin-sm (1rem)

## Elevation

No drop shadows or blur as primary depth cues. Depth via tonal planes and 1px hairline edges (`#e2e8f0`, `#e5e7eb`). Interactive states shift border stroke to `#0f172a` (charcoal) or `#059669` (emerald) without translating on Y-axis.

## Radius

- Default (0.25rem / 4px): inputs, buttons, pills, thumbnails
- Container (0.5rem / 8px): modals, flyout carts, checkout panels
- Pills (9999px): live inventory indicators

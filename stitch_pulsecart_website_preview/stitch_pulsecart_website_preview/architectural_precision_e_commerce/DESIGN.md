## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a clinical, high-speed, and structurally disciplined e-commerce environment. Rooted in Architectural Minimalism, it strips away decorative consumer-facing chrome in favor of immaculate spatial composition, hairline boundaries, and data-dense operational precision.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Clinical & Measured:** Every element behaves like an architectural blueprint or inventory terminal—direct, silent, and devoid of frivolous ornamentation.
- **High-Velocity Utility:** Geared toward discerning buyers, algorithmic procurement, and collectors who prioritize instant legibility, sub-millisecond stock confirmation, and zero ambiguity.
- **Quiet Authority:** Premium positioning achieved through severe structural discipline, abundant calculated white space, and unapologetic contrast.

### Design Principles
1. **Structural Purity:** Division is achieved through hairline spatial dividers rather than heavy fills or diffused drop shadows.
2. **Tabular Truth:** Numeric values (pricing, SKU data, inventory latency counters) occupy equal-width tabular slots to maintain rock-solid alignment during live data streaming.
3. **Restrained Functional Chroma:** Color is never decorative; it exists purely to signal real-time state changes, active telemetry, and high-priority transaction milestones.

## Colors

The palette operates on an austere, light-mode register evoking sterile laboratory porcelain and wet ink on drafting paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#ffffff`, `#f8f9fa`):** Porcelain and cold alabaster foundations that keep the viewport weightless and stark.
- **Deep Ink Typographic Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Content / Headers: Deep Charcoal Ink (`#0f172a`).
  - Secondary Content / Metadata: Slated Muted Charcoal (`#334155`).
  - Tertiary / Disabled / Captions: Neutral Slate (`#64748b`).
- **Hairline Framing (`#e2e8f0`, `#e5e7eb`):** Single-pixel perimeter strokes that articulate layout bounding boxes without adding visual bulk.
- **Micro-Telemetry Accents:**
  - **Terminal Emerald (`#059669`):** Reserved for active stock status, positive order confirmation, and real-time connectivity indicators.
  - **Precision Cyan (`#0891b2`):** Used for micro-badges, tabular updates, and focused interactive states.

### Contrast Discipline
Surface containers rely on clean white cutouts overlaid upon cold off-white canvases. Avoid gradient fills or blurred color underlays. All critical text exceeds a 7:1 contrast ratio against default backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographic engine uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly to create clean geometric cohesion.

### Tabular Formatting
All numeric data streams—including checkout totals, latency readouts, SKU serials, stock countdowns, and dynamic order quantities—must enforce OpenType tabular figures:
```css
font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1;
font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;
```
This guarantees vertical glyph alignment within catalog tables, receipt manifests, and fast-updating shopping cart modules.

### Treatment & Hierarchy
- Headlines leverage tight tracking to produce an editorial, architectural silhouette.
- Micro-labels (`label-sm`) default to uppercase styling with slight letter tracking (`+0.04em`) to act as technical category tags, breadcrumb paths, or status badges.
- Body copy prioritizes airy line-heights to balance the austerity of the surrounding borders.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are treated as structural frameworks built on a disciplined 12-column modular grid with edge-to-edge mathematical snapping.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px), `margin-lg` (3rem / 48px). Maximum layout container width: 1440px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px), `margin` (2rem / 32px).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px), `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px).

### Architectural Grid Rules
- Content zones snap cleanly to hairline axes. Rather than floating cards inside arbitrary white margins, views often use contiguous dividing grids where cards touch along a single 1px `#e2e8f0` structural seam.
- Spacing relies on uniform intervals: inner paddings use `space-md` or `space-lg`, while dense technical line items snap to `space-sm` for compact reading.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects traditional drop shadows and blur-heavy depth cues. Visual depth is established purely through **tonal planes** and **hairline edge definition**.

### Edge-Defined Layering
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Pure `#ffffff` or `#f8f9fa` surface.
- **Structural Outlines:** All panel perimeters, popovers, and line items rely on crisp `1px solid #e2e8f0` or `#e5e7eb` framing.
- **Overlays & Drawers:** Slide-over shopping carts and modal inspectors do not use ambient atmospheric drop shadows. Instead, they use a high-precision `1px solid #0f172a` boundary line coupled with a muted, neutral scrim (`#0f172a` at 12% opacity) to isolate the interaction context.
- **Interactive Focus:** Hovered products and focused form fields shift boundary strokes from `#e2e8f0` to sharp Charcoal (`#0f172a`) or micro-emerald (`#059669`) without altering element dimensions or casting shadows.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a severe, near-rectilinear profile with soft technical softening (Level 1).

### Radius Allocation
- **Default Elements (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form inputs, buttons, inventory pill trackers, notification banners, and thumbnail frames.
- **Container Surfaces (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Modals, flyout carts, checkout summary panels, and mega-menu trays (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pills / Status Dots (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for live inventory micro-indicators (e.g., green stock status dots, latency counters).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0f172a`, text `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #0f172a`, radius `0.25rem`. Sharp hover transition shifting to `#1e293b`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#0f172a`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, radius `0.25rem`. Hover shifts border to `#0f172a`.
- **Action Metric:** Compact button containing tabular price integration on the trailing edge, separated by an interior vertical hairline stroke (`#334155`).

### Input Fields
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`.
- Text styled in `body-md` (`#0f172a`). Placeholder styled in `#94a3b8`.
- Focus state: Border color snaps directly to `#0f172a` with zero glow/shadow rings (`outline: none`).

### Product & Data Cards
- Pure white face, `1px solid #e2e8f0` border, `0.25rem` radius.
- Imagery framed with edge-to-edge hairline bottom dividers.
- Hover state: Border stroke shifts to `#0f172a`. Card does not translate on the Y-axis.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Height: 22px, padding: `0 0.5rem`, radius `0.25rem`.
- Monochromatic or micro-accented:
  - In Stock: `#f0fdf4` fill, `#059669` label, hairline `#bbf7d0` border.
  - Latency / Telemetry: `#f0fdfa` fill, `#0891b2` label with leading 6px circular dot indicator.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 16x16px. Border: `1px solid #cbd5e1`. Background: `#ffffff`.
- Checked state: `#0f172a` fill with crisp white vector mark. Instant state change without bouncy easing.

### Shopping Cart Flyout & Data Tables
- Integrated receipt layout with horizontal hairpins (`border-b: 1px solid #f1f5f9`).
- Numbers rendered with `tabular-data-md` to preserve clean tabular columns across quantities, unit costs, and subtotal metrics.
